02 — Intimate portraits / Ritual photography

Being photographed as being gently witnessed — not perfected.

The way we are looked at changes what becomes visible. I make a space where the camera's gaze feels less like observation and more like care.

Individuals, couples, lovers, friends. Movement, silence, nudity — not as an aesthetic goal, but as an invitation to feel at home in your own body. The photographs are traces. What matters most is the encounter itself.
